ENERGY

Crude Oil (WTI)

+0.08%
September WTI crude closed up +0.07 (+0.08%), though it was trading lower earlier at -0.19 (-0.23%) following the US crude inventory surge.

Key Drivers

  • International Energy Agency (IEA) forecasts pointed to tightening inventories in Q3; earlier pressure came from a US crude oil inventory build.

Reverse Factors

  • The US crude inventory surge remains a bearish supply-side factor; further inventory builds could reverse gains.
Short-term influencer - UP

Natural Gas

+1.34%

Key Drivers

  • Forecasts from Vaisala continued to call for hotter-than-average temperatures, supporting cooling demand.

Reverse Factors

  • Weather-driven demand is temporary; a shift toward cooler forecasts could quickly remove support.
Short-term influencer - UP

Gasoline (RBOB)

+0.55%

Key Drivers

Reverse Factors

  • The earlier US crude inventory build and potential demand softness could pressure gasoline prices.
Short-term influencer - UP

GRAINS

Wheat

Wheat rallied sharply; Chicago SRW contracts were 13 ¾ to 22 ½ cents higher, and KC HRW futures were 14 ½ to 23 cents higher across front months.

Key Drivers

  • Black Sea strikes fueled supply concerns and led the grain complex higher; open interest rose by 15,741 contracts, suggesting new buying.

Reverse Factors

  • If Black Sea tensions ease, wheat could give back gains quickly; early reports showed mixed action before the rally.
Short-term influencer - UP

Corn

Corn futures rallied after the USDA reports, with midday gains of 12 to 13 cents across most contracts; deferreds were up 4 to 11 ½ cents.

Key Drivers

  • USDA Crop Production and WASDE reports showed a smaller-than-expected yield and a cut to stocks; spillover support came from wheat and Black Sea strike concerns.

Reverse Factors

  • Open interest fell by 17,119 contracts, mainly in September contracts, suggesting rolling/positioning rather than fresh conviction.
Long-term influencer - UP

Soybeans

Soybeans closed with gains of 6 to 17 ¼ cents; the cmdtyView national average cash bean price rose 14 ¾ cents to $11.41.

Key Drivers

  • Prices held gains despite the USDA increasing US output in its first official crop estimate; early strength was also tied to pre-report positioning.

Reverse Factors

  • The USDA report was viewed as neutral to slightly bearish, and increased US production could limit upside.
Unclear

LIVESTOCK

Lean Hogs

Lean hog futures posted modest gains, with contracts 22 to 95 cents higher; soon-to-expire August was down 20 cents.

Key Drivers

  • USDA’s national base hog price was reported at $95.81 on Wednesday afternoon; August futures are nearing Friday expiration.

Reverse Factors

  • October lean hog futures recently hit a four-week low, and Tuesday’s open interest increase of 4,691 contracts suggested modest new selling.
Short-term influencer - UP

Live Cattle

Live cattle futures fell sharply, posting losses of $2.00 to $2.97 across the board; front-month losses were $1.50 to $2.50 in midday trading.

Key Drivers

  • Cash trade has been quiet this week; the Wednesday Fed Cattle auction showed no sales, and there were no deliveries issued against August futures.

Reverse Factors

  • Quiet cash trade means any stronger cash sales could support a rebound; no deliveries may also indicate tightening supply.
Short-term influencer - DOWN

Coffee

+1.30%
September arabica coffee closed up +4.35 (+1.30%), while September robusta coffee closed down -13 (-0.34%).

Reverse Factors
