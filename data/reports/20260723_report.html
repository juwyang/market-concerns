US Dollar Index

+0.35%
The dollar index (DXY00) rose by +0.35% on Thursday.

Key Drivers

  • Strong US interest rate differentials (10-year T-note yield rose +4 bp to a 1.5-year high), a Houthi tanker attack, and higher oil prices supported the dollar.

Reverse Factors

  • One article notes the dollar index can be a “mirage” when all components weaken together; a change in Fed expectations could alter the trend.
Short-term influencer – UP

Key Themes

  • **Geopolitical tension drives energy and dollar:** The US-Iran conflict expansion to the Red Sea and a Houthi tanker attack propelled oil prices sharply higher and boosted the safe-haven dollar.
  • **Grains show mixed performance:** Corn and soybeans gained on weather and speculative bets, while wheat reversed after hitting multi-year highs. Soybeans lacked a clear fundamental driver.
  • **Softs broadly lower on supply-side factors:** Cocoa, coffee, and sugar all fell due to abundant supplies and improved monsoon rains, with coffee pressured by a USDA production forecast.
  • **Livestock market bounces:** Cattle recovered from recent lows on cash trade support, while hogs posted front-month gains amid firm hog prices.

Risks Highlighted

  • Escalation of the US-Iran conflict in the Red Sea could further disrupt oil supplies and heighten global risk.
  • Persistent oversupply in cocoa and coffee markets risks sustained price weakness.
  • A stronger dollar may continue to weigh on commodity export prices, especially for softs and grains.
  • Profit-taking after oil’s sharp rally could pull prices back from multi-year highs.
  • Weather shifts (e.g., monsoon progress in India, US forecast changes) could alter supply expectations for sugar and grains.
