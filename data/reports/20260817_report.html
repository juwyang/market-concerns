GRAINS

Corn

Corn futures held Monday gains into the close, with contracts 3 to 6 1/4 cents higher across most months. Cash corn was up 6 1/4 cents at $4.36 1/4. Early/midday trade showed gains of 1 3/4 to 5 1/2 cents.

Key Drivers

  • USDA trimmed crop condition ratings in Monday’s Crop Progress report. The annual Pro Farmer Crop Tour also kicked off, acting as a major catalyst for corn prices.

Reverse Factors

  • The Crop Tour is seeing a “split crop,” meaning scouts could find better-than-expected yields. Profit-taking after the rally is also possible.
Short-term influencer - UP

Soybeans & Soymeal

Soybeans rallied on Monday with gains of 12 to 23 1/2 cents across the board. The national average cash bean price rose 24 1/4 cents to $11.75 1/4. Soymeal futures also closed higher, up $1.10 to $2.40.

Key Drivers

  • Soybean condition ratings slipped in the latest USDA Crop Progress data. The Pro Farmer Crop Tour is also adding supply uncertainty to the market.

Reverse Factors

  • If Crop Tour scouts see better-than-expected soybean conditions, prices could fade. Profit-taking after recent gains is also a risk.
Short-term influencer - UP

Wheat
