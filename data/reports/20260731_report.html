GRAINS

Wheat

Wheat declined to close out July, with Chicago SRW contracts down 18 ¼ to 24 ¼ cents at the close. Intraday losses were as large as 27-28 cents.

Key Drivers

  • Month-end money came out of positions; wheat gave back Thursday’s gains across the three exchanges.

Reverse Factors

  • Thursday’s gains were tied to increased Black Sea tensions, and those geopolitical concerns could re-emerge.
Short-term influencer - DOWN

Soybeans

Key Drivers

  • The soybean article highlighted week-end weakness; broader grain-market pressure was linked to wheat spillover and month-end position squaring.

Reverse Factors

  • The national average cash bean price was up 8 ½ cents at $11.40, showing cash-market strength despite futures weakness.
Short-term influencer - DOWN
